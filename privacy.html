Privacy Policy

Effective 9 October 2026 · Applies to AISub 2.1.0 and later

Short version: AISub has no account, no telemetry and no analytics. Your video and subtitle files are never uploaded anywhere. The only data that leaves your Mac is the subtitle text you chose to translate, and that goes to the AI provider you configured.

1. What AISub does not do

The app contains no telemetry, no analytics SDK, no crash reporting service and no advertising identifier. It does not collect usage statistics and it does not phone home. The only network requests the app makes are the two described below.

2. The two network requests

RequestWhenWhat is sent
Your AI endpoint Only when you start a translation job The subtitle lines in the current batch, plus a few lines of preceding context so the translation reads naturally. Never the video, never the file path, never your filesystem.
GitHub Releases API Once at launch, and when you check for updates A normal HTTPS GET for the public release metadata. No personal data is attached.

The app also runs a connectivity test when you press the connection test button. That sends the fixed string Reply with exactly: OK so the API key can be verified before a long job starts. No subtitle content is involved.

3. Who receives your subtitle text

AISub ships with https://api.anthropic.com as the default endpoint, but you can change it to any Anthropic-compatible address, including a local proxy or a self-hosted model server. Whatever you set is what receives the text.

Once the text leaves your Mac, it is governed by that provider's privacy policy, not by this one. If that matters to you, point the app at a local endpoint such as Ollama or a proxy you control. We never see, log or store your subtitle content.

Copyright reminder: translating subtitles from a film or series is something you do for personal use. AISub does not grant you any rights to distribute translated copies of copyrighted material. See Terms of Use.

4. What stays on your Mac

  • Your files. Videos and generated .srt files are read and written entirely on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Your API key. Stored in the macOS Keychain under the service name com.zhengxie.aisub, never in a preferences file. This is deliberate: a plist in ~/Library/Preferences can be read by any process on the machine and swept up by backups. If you used an older build that stored the key in plain text, AISub migrates it into the Keychain on first launch and deletes the old copy. You can verify this by searching for apiKey in defaults read com.zhengxie.aisub — it should not appear.
  • Your settings. Language, batch size, theme and tool paths are stored in standard macOS UserDefaults under com.zhengxie.aisub. These are preferences only and contain nothing sensitive.
  • Resume progress. If you enable checkpointing, translation progress is written to a file next to your output so an interrupted job can continue. Delete it whenever you like.

5. Local subprocesses

AISub shells out to ffmpeg and ffprobe to read the embedded subtitle track out of your video. These are the same tools you install from Homebrew for any video work. AISub never uploads files itself, and it does not modify or delete your originals.

ffmpeg is not bundled. It must already be on your system, for example brew install ffmpeg. The path is configurable in Settings if it lives somewhere unusual.

6. Third-party services

  • Your AI provider — receives subtitle text as described in section 3.
  • GitHub — serves the release files you download and the update check. Your download is an ordinary request to a public CDN.
  • ffmpeg — runs locally on your machine. It makes no network requests.

We do not embed advertising trackers, session replay tools or third-party pixels anywhere in AISub or on this website.

7. Children

AISub is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. Because we collect no personal data at all, there is nothing about a user to collect.

8. Your rights

Because AISub collects no personal data, there is nothing stored on our side to export, correct or delete. You can remove everything AISub leaves on your Mac by deleting the app, or by running:

  • defaults delete com.zhengxie.aisub to clear settings
  • Keychain Access → search com.zhengxie.aisub to delete the stored API key

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top is updated and the new version is published at this URL. Material changes will also be noted in the app's changelog.
